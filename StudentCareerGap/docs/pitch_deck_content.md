# Pitch Deck Content

## Slide 1 — Problem statement
Students do not know which skills are needed for target roles and cannot find trusted free learning paths.

## Slide 2 — Target users and pain points
Students preparing for placements, internships, and career transitions with limited time and fragmented guidance.

## Slide 3 — Research and existing solutions
Most students rely on static job descriptions, manual internet research, and unstructured advice.

## Slide 4 — Proposed SkillPath AI solution
A transparent career-readiness dashboard that matches skills to job requirements and builds a learning plan.

## Slide 5 — User workflow and system architecture
Profile → Assess → Match → Gap analysis → Learning resources → Weekly plan → Progress tracking → Report export.

## Slide 6 — AI tools and recommendation methodology
Rule-based recommendation engine, skill weighting, explanation templates, and a local SQLite data store.

## Slide 7 — Demo and sample results
The demo student shows a Data Analyst path with skill gap recommendations and a generated study plan.

## Slide 8 — Feasibility, impact, and future scope
Low-cost, open-source, local-first deployment with real student adoption potential and future AI enhancements.
