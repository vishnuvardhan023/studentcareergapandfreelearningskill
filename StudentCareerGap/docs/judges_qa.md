# Judges' Q&A

## 1. What problem does SkillPath AI solve?
It helps students understand job-role expectations, find missing skills, and create a free learning plan.

## 2. Why is this useful for placements?
It gives a practical path from current skill level to target roles without vague advice.

## 3. Does it use paid AI APIs?
No. The app relies on a rule-based recommendation engine and transparent templates.

## 4. Is it secure?
The app stores local student data in SQLite and does not expose public profiles.

## 5. Can students use it without an external AI model?
Yes. The app works fully without external AI services.

## 6. How are recommendations generated?
The system matches current skill ratings against role requirements with weighted scoring.

## 7. Can it handle multiple roles?
Yes. The student can compare multiple targeted roles and see match percentages.

## 8. Are the resources really free?
They come from official free learning pages and are clearly labeled with free-access status.

## 9. What if a student has zero study hours?
The app still loads and alerts the student that the plan may need adjustment.

## 10. Can learning plans be regenerated?
Yes. Students can generate a new plan any time based on gaps and selected role.

## 11. Is the sample data clearly separated?
Yes. The demo student and role profiles are labeled as illustrative sample data.

## 12. Can users download reports?
Yes. CSV and PDF reports are available from the Download Reports page.

## 13. What are the main technical components?
Python, Streamlit, SQLite, Plotly, and ReportLab.

## 14. What is the biggest differentiator?
It combines assessment, gap analysis, resource discovery, planning, and tracking in one app.

## 15. What is the future scope?
The platform could add richer role libraries, mentor support, and deeper AI-assisted explanations later.
