# Research Summary

## Target users

The platform is designed for undergraduate and graduate students preparing for campus placements or internship transitions. Typical users are looking for role guidance, skill clarity, and free study resources.

## Pain points

- Unclear role requirements
- Difficulty identifying technical and soft-skill gaps
- Limited access to trustworthy free study resources
- Difficulty making a learning plan around limited study time

## Existing alternatives

Students often rely on static job descriptions, scattered YouTube videos, and informal advice from seniors. These methods are useful, but they are fragmented and not personalized to the student's actual profile.

## Differentiation

SkillPath AI gives students a single workflow for:

- assessing current skills
- comparing to job role profiles
- identifying missing skills
- finding free resources
- generating a personal weekly plan
- monitoring progress

This makes the platform practical, local-first, and easy to demo without paid tools or APIs.
